Metric definitions, assumptions & research basis

All operating numbers are fictional. The 300-site demonstration is not OXXO's actual footprint. Demo markets, districts, budgets, owners, thresholds, dates, projects and targets do not describe internal OXXO plans.

Site controllable contribution: inside gross profit + retail fuel gross profit − fully loaded store labor − payment fees − other controllable store expenses. Excludes occupancy/rent, depreciation, corporate allocations, interest and income taxes; not net income, EBITDA or free cash flow. Waste and shrink are already in inside cost of goods sold and are not deducted twice.

Fuel: retail fuel GP is net fuel sales less landed fuel cost, excluding fuel taxes; cents per gallon is total GP ÷ total gallons × 100, before payment fees. Inside sales exclude fuel, sales tax and lottery pass-throughs. Ratios are recomputed from totals, never averaged across stores.

Comparables: only the 240 fictional established stores qualify, subject to market/cohort filters; identical stores and equivalent 28-day periods are compared. Converted/new cohorts show N/A for these growth rates. Like-for-like sales growth = (1 + transaction growth) × (1 + basket growth) − 1.

Operations: dispenser uptime uses available ÷ scheduled dispenser-hours for sites with telemetry; availability uses available ÷ audited priority-item checks. Food waste is disposal cost ÷ food sales. Inside GP / labor hour uses all paid store hours and is not an individual productivity score. Local review flags do not contact anyone or resolve an issue.

Investment sensitivity: $2.6m annual inside sales × sales uplift × 36% incremental gross margin − $28k added annual operating cost. Fuel GP held flat. Payback = investment ÷ positive annual incremental contribution; no finite payback when contribution is zero or negative. These are scenario inputs, not forecasts or OXXO assumptions.

Research basis: FEMSA 2025 annual report (published March 2026): U.S. conversion and food/coffee adaptation; OXXO USA Growth & Real Estate: South-Central U.S. sites, chains and development; NACS, August 31, 2026: transaction pressure, labor productivity and inventory; Casey's, September 8, 2026: comp inside sales, gallons and fuel margin; Murphy USA, March 2026: investment cohorts and store ramp. Public research informs the design; no peer metric is treated as an OXXO target.
