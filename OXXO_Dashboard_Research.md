# OXXO USA leadership dashboard: research and design rationale

Prepared for Bijaya Humagain's Manager, Business Intelligence & Analytics interview. Research reviewed September 30, 2026.

The dashboard is an interview concept using **300 fictional stores**. Its numbers, store locations, projects, budgets, owners and targets are illustrative. It does not represent OXXO's actual footprint, financial performance, internal technology or approved expansion plan. The recommendations below are design inferences from public evidence.

## What the business evidence suggests

**Expansion needs a pipeline and consistent site economics.** OXXO USA's official Growth & Real Estate page seeks stores, small chains and land in the South-Central United States. Its submission form asks about fuel volumes, pumps, inside sales, foodservice and property restrictions. The proposed expansion view therefore separates diligence from approved capital, committed spend and forecast final cost, and keeps readiness blockers visible. [OXXO USA Growth & Real Estate](https://oxxo-us.com/growth-real-estate/)

**Conversions should be evaluated as investments.** FEMSA's 2025 operating-performance summary reports 54 U.S. stores converted to the OXXO brand. A conversion program calls for cohort tracking and results against the original business case, beyond counting openings. The concept separates established, converted and new locations; the growth view illustrates early contribution ramp and conversion investment sensitivity. [FEMSA 2025 operating performance](https://annualreport.femsa.com/operating-performance.html)

**Revenue can obscure traffic and profit pressure.** NACS' August 31, 2026 review reports that 2025 fuel sales dollars declined while gallons rose; inside transaction counts also fell. It highlights expense management, inside gross profit per labor hour, inventory and shrink. The dashboard therefore separates gallons from margin, transactions from basket size, and pairs productivity with item availability. [NACS: Profit Under Pressure](https://convenience.org/stay-current/conveniencecorner/state-of-the-c-store-industry-profit-under-pressure)

**Comparable-store results and expansion should remain distinct.** Casey's September 8, 2026 release separately reports comparable inside sales, comparable fuel gallons, total growth and new locations. Its retail fuel margin excludes credit card fees. The concept uses a fixed eligible same-store set and a clearly defined fuel margin; payment fees are deducted separately when calculating site contribution. Peer margins are not OXXO performance targets. [Casey's quarterly results](https://investor.caseys.com/news-releases/news-release-details/caseys-announces-first-quarter-results-3)

**Definitions and investment cohorts matter.** Murphy USA distinguishes retail fuel margin from broader fuel contribution in its 2025 results. Its March 2026 investor presentation tracks new-store classes and operating ramps. This supports a metric dictionary and cohort-based investment review. The concept's simplified contribution payback is expressly not ROIC or a full investment valuation. [Murphy USA FY2025 results](https://ir.corporate.murphyusa.com/investor-relations/news-releases/press-release-details/2026/Murphy-USA-Inc--Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance/default.aspx) · [March 2026 presentation](https://s22.q4cdn.com/506259022/files/doc_events/2026/Mar/02/MurphyUSA_2026InvestorPresentation.pdf)

## The four leadership decisions supported

1. **Where are we missing plan?** Contribution bridge, market results, store distribution and the lowest store variances identify where to investigate.
2. **What is driving the economics?** Fuel volume/margin, inside traffic/basket, category profit, food waste and loyalty-linkage coverage explain the commercial picture.
3. **Who needs to act next?** Operational evidence, suggested owners, dates and local review flags connect reporting to follow-up. These flags do not assign work or validate savings.
4. **Is expansion meeting its business case?** Conversion/new-store cohorts, capital exposure, readiness gates and transparent sensitivity assumptions support disciplined review.

## Interview talking points

- “I would start with a common definition of store contribution agreed with Finance, then explain its variance through volume, margin and controllable costs.”
- “For a growing network, I would separate established-store performance from conversions and new openings so expansion doesn't hide weaker traffic.”
- “Every exception should have evidence, an owner and a follow-up measure. Every investment should retain its original business case so we can learn from actual results.”

Before implementation, validate operating calendars, store eligibility, cost accruals, fuel cost timing, data coverage and ownership with Finance and Operations. Use matched comparisons for conversion evaluation; a before/after increase alone does not establish causation.
